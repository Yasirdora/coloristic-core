# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Coloristic Core is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free project. Be considerate, assume good intent while addressing impact, give actionable feedback, and keep technical disagreement focused on the work.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, deliberate disruption, publishing another person's private information, and sexualized attention or content.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Until a dedicated conduct address is published, report concerns through the repository's [private advisory form](https://github.com/Yasirdora/coloristic-core/security/advisories/new) and prefix the title with `[Conduct]`. This route is used solely to provide a private maintainer channel; a conduct report does not need to describe a software vulnerability.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, or harmful. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and impartially as practical, with privacy respected to the extent possible.
